- **Type an idea** and get a video back.
- **Bring a picture to life** by using it as the first frame.
- **Set where it starts and ends**, and let the model fill in the motion.
- **Add sound** with generated audio.

Each model brings its own strengths, so you choose the one that fits the job.

## Make videos by chatting with an agent

You don't have to set any of this up yourself. In the Antseed AI VPN, you can ask the chat agent for a video, and it takes care of the rest.

Tell the agent what you want. It will:

- find the video models available right now,
- check what each provider supports and charges,
- ask only the questions that matter, like length, resolution, and shape,
- use images you upload or generate in the chat as first and last frames,
- draft a script and storyboard when you want one, and
- play the finished video right in the chat.

Before any paid video starts, the agent shows you the model, your choices, the provider, and the estimated price, then waits for your OK.

People use it to go from a rough idea to a finished clip without writing a prompt for every step. Try something like:

> "Create a short launch video for my app. Write the script, make the first and last frames, then use the best available video model for a 10-second video."

For longer videos, the agent plans a series of connected clips. It shows you the plan and the total price, makes the clips one at a time, then joins them into one video. When the model supports it, each clip starts from where the last one ended, so the cuts line up.
