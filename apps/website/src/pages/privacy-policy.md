# Antseed - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 16, 2026**

## We don't store your data

There is no Antseed account to create. No Antseed server holds your prompts, chats, files or keys. They stay on your device, or on the gateway you run yourself.

## What leaves your device, and to whom

- **Your requests go to the provider you choose.** That provider sees what it serves, as with any AI service. Payments settle publicly on the Base blockchain.
- **If you run a gateway,** its operator (you or your organization) controls its data, not Antseed.
- **Card payments** are handled by the payment provider: Crossmint, or Stripe via Outerfound in the US.
- **Like any network software,** the app connects to public services (peer discovery, the Base blockchain, update checks), which see your IP address.

## The only data we collect

- **Website analytics.** When you visit antseed.com or download the app, Google Analytics records pages viewed, clicks and downloads, using cookies. Google uses your IP address to estimate your location; our own download records don't include it. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Product telemetry, only if you turn it on.** It is off by default. If you turn it on, the app sends usage and reliability counts (for example, whether requests succeed and how fast they are), never your prompts or responses. Turn it off anytime in the desktop app under Preferences → Privacy, in the gateway console under Settings → Privacy, or with `antseed telemetry disable`. [Full details](https://github.com/Antseed/antseed/blob/main/docs/telemetry.md).

We don't sell personal information.
