How it works

Many machines.
One Colony.

A Colony brings people’s computers together to serve AI on Antseed. To everyone using Antseed, it is one provider.

Your Mac joins. Antseed Colony is a menu bar app. Your Mac joins a Colony and downloads the model it runs.

The Colony works as one provider. Each request goes to a Mac in the Colony that can answer it.

Payments settle onchain. The Colony’s contract splits each settlement between its Macs.

You claim. The app shows what your Mac has earned. Claim it to your own wallet when you like.

What is
a Colony?

18 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

A Colony is a group of machines working together to serve AI on Antseed.

An operator coordinates the Colony, and each machine is paid for the answers it gives. To everyone using Antseed, it is one provider. Read the docs →

Who can join

Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 14 or later. The first Colony serves a large model, so it starts with Mac Studios with 256 GB or more.

  • Pause sharing from the menu bar any time.
  • Joining is free.
  • Claim what your Mac earns to your own wallet.
