# Antseed

**Your job is to reproduce this system exactly, not to reinterpret it.** The dot-matrix titles, the green eyebrows, the mono details, the acid light, the arrows on links and the dotted margins are deliberate. Do not invent a colour, size, radius, spacing, shadow or component. If a page needs something this file does not have, build it from the pieces below; if that is impossible, stop and ask (see Open items).

The kit, all paths relative to this file: `@antseed.css` (every value below, as classes; link it, never retype it), `@antseed.js` (theme, menus, 8px rounding), `@starter.html` (every component, marked `<!-- COMPONENT: name -->`; copy from it), `effects/` (the paintings' LED arrival and mosaic), `fonts/`, `logo/`, `@voice.md` (words), `@mascot.md` (the ant), `@watercolour.md` (paintings). Paintings are not in the kit: reference them as `assets/paintings/<name>-day.webp` and `assets/paintings/<name>-night.webp`, or make new ones with `@watercolour.md` and the images in `refs/`.

## Overview

A developer product page printed on watercolour paper. White **rails** sit on a page-wide **dotted margin**; each section is one stretch of rail outlined in round dots. Titles are set in a face made of dots, in two tones. One filled green button per row of actions. Paintings are sparse watercolour landscapes in rounded panels, each with its own night.

Four ideas hold it: the dot is the ant (margins, outlines, titles and arrivals are dots); nothing has a centre (no hub layouts); the seed is the request; day and night are two paintings, never a filter. New pages are built like the subpages (/colony/, /network/, /providers/, /ants/, /ecosystem/): the full-width nav, then sections on the dotted margin, then the closing band and the footer. The homepage's hero card and its 90/93 H1 are homepage-only.

**The dotted margin** is `.s-page`'s background: `radial-gradient(circle at 4px 4px, var(--color-dot) .85px, #0000 1.25px) 50% -4px / 8px 8px, var(--color-page)`, a 2.5px soft round dot every 8px, one column on the page's centre line, one row on `.s-page`'s top edge. Because the rail is a multiple of 16 and centred, its edges land on dot columns.

**The dotted outline** of every rail (and of FAQ rows, steps cells, the legal row, quotes) is an overlay, never `border: dotted`:

```css
.s-rail::before { content: ""; position: absolute; inset: -1px; z-index: -1; pointer-events: none;
  --d: radial-gradient(circle at 1px 1px, var(--color-dot) .85px, #0000 1.25px);
  background: var(--d) 0 0 / 8px var(--dt) repeat-x, var(--d) 100% 0 / var(--dr) 8px repeat-y,
              var(--d) 0 100% / 8px var(--db) repeat-x, var(--d) 0 0 / var(--dl) 8px repeat-y; }
.s-rail { --dt: 2px; --dr: 2px; --db: 0px; --dl: 2px; }   /* top, right, left on; the next section draws the bottom */
```

A side is on when its variable is `2px`, off at `0px`: 2px round dots on an 8px pitch, the first on the corner.

**The 8px grid.** Every section's height is a multiple of 8, so each top and its dotted line sit on a row of margin dots. The page hero is `round(down, calc(100svh - 69px), 8px)` tall from 1200px (824 at 1440 × 900). Every other section gets `min-height` rounded up to the next multiple of 8 by antseed.js (`Math.ceil(h / 8 - 0.02) * 8`, re-run on resize and after fonts load). The closing band and the footer are not rounded, as on the site. Sections touch: no margin between them.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat: paper, rails, dots. Depth exists in four places only, each with its exact shadow.

## Motion & Micro-interaction

| What | Property | Duration and easing |
|---|---|---|
| Button, link, field, social, footer switch | background, color | .15s ease |
| Eyebrow `›` on hover (only on `a.s-eyebrow`) | translateX(3px) | .2s ease |
| FAQ sign, + to − | the vertical bar rotates 90° | .2s ease |
| FAQ answer opens | `::details-content` height | .25s ease |
| Nav switch knob | translate 24px | .25s cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .2, 1) |
| Providers chevron | rotate 180° | .25s cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) |
| Link card picture | scale 1 → 1.02 | .3s ease-out |
| Theme change | whole-page crossfade (`document.startViewTransition`) | browser default |

No pressed movement, no bounce, no loops for attention. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every transition is off.

**Dotted effects are never hand-made.** The paintings' two effects ship in `effects/`, the site's own code (`card-unveil.js` and `wash-led.js`) with the same algorithms, values and timings. Load them at the end of `<body>`, as starter.html does:

```html
<script type="module" src="effects/arrival.js"></script>
<script type="module" src="effects/mosaic.js"></script>
```

They take every `.wash` in the page that carries `--wash` (page hero, two-option, card and post-card pictures, duo, steps, article figure; the footer's meadow is an `<img>` and takes neither), reading `--wash`, `--wash-d` and `--wash-pos`. To keep a painting out, set `data-effects="none"` on its `.wash` (shown at once, no mosaic); `data-effects="arrival"` keeps only the arrival, `data-effects="mosaic"` only the mosaic.

- **LED arrival** (`arrival.js`): each painting arrives once, as it comes into view. Until then it carries `data-unveil` (`wait`, `soon`, `run`) and antseed.css shows the panel plain in `--color-wash`, its glass hidden. Then the painting grows from the centre in an oval; the moving edge breaks into round dots on the 8px grid, scattering further out, and just inside it the painting fills in cell by cell. At the end the canvas and `data-unveil` are gone and the panel is as before.
- **Mosaic under the cursor** (`mosaic.js`): over an arrived painting, the picture breaks into the same round dots where the pointer passes, each in the colour of the painting under it, with the rail's paper coming up round them toward the trail's heart. At the trail's edge the painting returns as flat squares. The trail fades and the painting is whole again.

| Parameter | Arrival | Mosaic |
|---|---|---|
| Grid · dot | 8px · radius 3.36px (0.42 × 8) | 8px · radius 3.36px (0.42 × 8) |
| Starts | 30% of the painting in view and showing, then 150ms and its file (day or night) decoded; paintings coming in together 160ms apart | a mouse over the painting once it has arrived |
| Runs | 1500ms, `1 − (1 − t)³` (ease-out cubic); the oval keeps the panel's proportions, its edge goes from 0 to 1.36 (1 reaches the corners) | the trail is 88px in radius around the pointer, smoothstep falloff, laid every half cell along its path |
| Edge | a band 0.34 wide; a cell shows its dot when its random value ≤ depth^1.6; in the band's inner 0.22 the painting comes in as whole cells | per cell, trail level + its jitter (±0.11 random, ±0.15 value noise over 5 cells, in full from level 0.33): under 0.46 the painting, to 0.5 a flat square, from 0.5 a dot with the paper (`--color-rail`) rising round it in four steps of 25% between 0.5 and 0.8, from 1.06 bare paper |
| Ends | glass windows: opacity 0 → 1 over .55s ease-out, scale .9 → 1 over .9s `cubic-bezier(.2, .9, .25, 1.12)`, from the start of the run | halves every 0.3s; a cell under 0.02 is clear; cleared at once off screen or in a hidden tab |
| Colour | the cover is the panel's `--color-wash` | the painting averaged over the cell (cover at `--wash-pos`), contrast 1.1, saturation 1.2; at night 12% toward white |

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` neither runs: the paintings are there from the start and nothing follows the pointer. `?still` in the URL stops the arrival only, for captures and the self-check. On touch (no `(hover: hover)`, or a touch or pen pointer) there is no mosaic; the arrival still plays. A theme change mid-arrival recolours the cover; the mosaic reads the other painting on the next hover. Serve the page over http(s): Chrome refuses module scripts from file://, so there neither effect runs. The mosaic reads the painting's pixels, which a canvas allows only for images on the page's own origin; a painting from another origin shows no trail (and no error).

The closing band's dot field and the footer's films still come from the site's scripts in the client repo, `apps/website/static/site/hero/`, loaded as the site's pages load them (`colony/index.html`, last `<script type="module">`), with `.s-page` wrapped in `<div id="sections">` as in starter.html: `dither.js` (`privacyDots(card, { fit: true, step: 16 })` draws the closing band's dot field into `.cta-card .tw`; also the sky dither), `night-loop.js` (footer films), `fireflies.js` (footer fireflies at night), `micro.js`. Do: load them and `effects/`. Don't: draw dots, dither or LED effects with CSS, canvas code of your own, or an image model.

## Voice & Content Strategy

Words follow `@voice.md`. ## Page recipes

Every page: `<head>` as starter.html (charset, viewport, title "Page name · Antseed", `antseed.js` without defer, favicon `logo/favicon.svg`, the font links, `antseed.css`), then nav, `#sections > .s-page`, sections, closing band, footer, then the two `effects/` scripts (`<script type="module">`) before `</body>`.

- **Product or topic page:** page hero → intro → two-option or duo → card row → steps → FAQ → closing band → footer.
- **Blog index:** page hero (H1 a title sentence with its full stop, e.g. "Notes from / the network.") → one card-row section of post cards (as many rows as needed, 64 apart, then `.s-more`) → closing band → footer.
- **Page head without a painting:** when a page has no hero painting, its first section is a plain rail with a `.s-head` holding the eyebrow, `<h1 class="s-h1">` and the sub (the section-head spacing: 24, 16).
- **Featured item** (a featured post, product or story): a duo, painting first, the meta line in the eyebrow slot, an `.s-h2` title in two tones, the sub, then one `.s-link` ("Read the post →").
- **Article:** page hero (eyebrow = topic, H1 = title, sub = one-line summary; the meta line goes under the H1 as `<p class="s-meta">`) → a rail section with `.s-article` → card row "more posts" → closing band → footer.
- **Sign-up or contact:** page hero → a section head with `.s-form` under it (40 below) → FAQ → closing band → footer.

Alternate painting sides from one duo to the next. Keep one H1 per page.
