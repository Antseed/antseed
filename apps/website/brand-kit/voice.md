# Antseed voice

Open this to write anything: a title, a button, a post, a caption. DESIGN.md comes first. Antseed's team owns the copy: reuse the site's lines before writing new ones, and mark any new line as a draft for them.

## How it sounds

The friendly half lives in the ant, the motion and the paintings, so the words stay straight: plain, concrete, short, in the second person. The ant does the jokes; the sentence beside it never does.

- **Plain and concrete.** Each sentence says what happens. "Your device finds providers over Mainline DHT, the same network BitTorrent uses, talks to them directly, and pays per request. Nobody sits in the middle."
- **Second person.** Your agents, your computer, you pay less.
- **Technical ideas through things techies know.** "Like BitTorrent, but for AI."
- **Reassurance in a short clause.** "but you never have to touch it", "nothing to set up".
- **Short.** A subtitle is one or two sentences, a feature one line, a caption a name and one sentence.

## Patterns

- **H1:** on the homepage, a name, not a sentence, two tones, no full stop: "The open / intelligence network". On every other page the H1 is a title sentence like a section title, with a full stop: "Join the first / Mac Colony." · "Explore the / Antseed ecosystem."
- **Section title:** a statement, then its completion in gray, with a full stop. Sentence case. "Run your own / AI router." · "Ask anything, / on any model." · "ANTS is / liquid intelligence."
- **Eyebrow:** the product or the topic, then " ›", linking to its docs page. "Antseed Gateway ›" · "How the network works ›".
- **Feature line:** one idea, starting with the verb or the benefit, no full stop. "Access to 700+ models at lower prices".
- **Caption:** a name, then one sentence with a full stop. "**Pay per request** Every payment settles in USDC on Base."
- **Buttons:** a verb and two or three words. One filled, then ghosts; the second is "Learn more" or a docs name. "Get Antseed Desktop" · "Become a provider" · "Join Colony" · "Read the lightpaper".
- **Arrows:** → to the docs or a dialog in running text, ↗ to another site, › after an eyebrow.
- **Meta line:** a middle dot between parts. "1.04B cap · weekly epochs · burns".
- **A post:** one or two of the site's sentences, then the link. A post about a product reuses its section: eyebrow, title, the section's button in the post's text, never on the image. No "new", "now available", version or price.

## Names

- **Antseed**, one word, capital A only (ANTSEED is the wordmark's lettering, never text). The organization is Antseed Foundation.
- **Antseed Desktop** ("Download Desktop" in the nav, "Desktop app" on the hero's tab). Runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.
- **Antseed Gateway**, "the gateway" in running text, "Set Up Gateway" in the nav (as the live nav reads; the one title-case label). A command-line tool.
- **Colony**, "Join Colony".
- **$ANTS** for the token, **ANTS** for the unit: "10.5M ANTS burned".
- **Private**, capitalized as Antseed Desktop's mode: "Go Private".
- **AntSeedStats**, as written. **USDC on Base**, **TEE**, **Mainline DHT**, **BitTorrent**, as written.
- Model names as AntSeedStats writes them on a card ("Claude Opus 5.5"); lowercase API ids inside app windows ("kimi-k3.1-pro").

## Words

- Say **network**, not "market", in the one-liner.
- Say what is absent: **"Nobody sits in the middle."** Never draw or name a hub.
- Crypto is named only to say you don't need it: "Payments settle in USDC on Base, but you never have to touch it." Never "web3"; avoid "decentralized".
- The site's own words: peer-to-peer, onchain, settle, request, provider, buyer, API key, endpoint, open models, signed responses.
- Lines that can be reused as they are: "The open intelligence network", "Like BitTorrent, but for AI.", "Private by design.", "Nobody sits in the middle."

## Numbers

- Thousands with commas: 19,252. Large counts with one decimal and a letter: 742.8B, 10.5M. In a sentence, spelled out: "a hard cap of 1.04 billion".
- A round number and a plus in a title: 700+.
- Money: $2.4M, $20. Prices: two decimals from $0.10 up, two significant digits below, then the unit: $0.0031 /M, /image, /s.
- Savings with a true minus: −98%. Token amounts: 0.0021 USDC. Epochs: Epoch 30. Hashes and addresses: 0x7a…3f.
- On screen: "Geist Pixel Var" (the kit's font file), counting up once when seen.
- Every figure comes from AntSeedStats (CC BY 4.0): credit it with the date read, "Data: AntSeedStats, 16 Nov 2026". A figure typed by hand goes stale; read it again before anything ships. Never invent one.

## Spelling and punctuation

American spelling ("centralized"). Sentence case everywhere. Contractions are fine. An ampersand only in a short label ("Models & prices"). No exclamation marks, no emoji, no jokes or puns in the words.
