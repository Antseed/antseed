What we’re building

The world’s largest inference liquidity network.

Why it matters. Intelligence can be produced anywhere, so its market should be open to everyone. Anyone can supply inference directly to buyers, on their own terms.

Where ANTS fits. Inference is the currency of intelligence. ANTS makes it liquid.

A fully peer-to-peer network.
In three connected layers.

Many users and many independent providers, connected directly. No central Antseed server.

USDC pays.
$ANTS makes inference liquid.

USDC pays for the work.

  • Payments always use USDC, whether you use the Gateway, Desktop or CLI.
  • A budget is reserved before the first request; after each response you sign the running total.
  • The provider settles on Base and the unused budget returns to you.

Trust you can check.
A name is not enough.

Your device scores every provider from public signals and skips the ones below your bar. Independent auditors check that a provider serves the model it claims.

  • History Completed channels and settled USDC volume.
  • Model audits Knowledge Boundary Fingerprinting compares answers with the genuine model. Evidence is public.
  • Backing ANTS locked behind the provider’s identity.
  • Identity A signed peer identity, and a verified GitHub account or domain.

Our vision

Every computer a provider.
Every agent a customer.

As open models get cheaper to run, more people and companies will run AI on their own computers and servers. A single Mac, a GPU fleet, a pool of Macs like Colony or another whole network can each join as a provider. Antseed is the network where they find buyers.

Open. Anyone can buy or provide intelligence.

Direct. Buyers connect straight to providers, who set their own offers and prices.

Earned trust. Providers build reputation through the quality of their service.

Privacy. People choose the level of privacy they need.
