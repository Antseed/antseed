A hard cap
of 1.04 billion.

No minting beyond emissions and no admin mint. Emissions run in weekly epochs, and unused budget can be burned.

  • 5M ANTS an epoch at first, halving every 104 epochs
  • Up to 40% to stakers, 20% to usage, 10% to verification
  • Unused budget burns, up to 30% of each epoch

Stake behind
a provider.

Only settled, buyer-authorised payments count, weighted by the ANTS staked behind the provider. Rewards follow recognised usage, inside capped ranges.

  • Locked for the epochs you choose, from the next one
  • Stakers’ share grows from 2% toward 40% of emissions
  • Leaving early burns 5 to 50% of the stake, not rewards
